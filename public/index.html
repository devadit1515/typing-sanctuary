Three moves, end to end.

01

A burst of typing becomes a fingerprint

Each keystroke carries four timings — how long it is held, and the gaps around it. A small neural network reduces one burst to 128 numbers.

02

Your fingerprints cluster; strangers’ sit apart

Trained the way face recognition is trained, the same person’s samples land close together. About a dozen samples enrol you, with no retraining.

03

Distance decides, and failure asks for another factor

Three classical distance measures judge each new sample against your cluster. When anything breaks, the answer is never “let them in”.

There is also a typing game — races, friends, leaderboards. It is where the consented keystroke data comes from, and it is genuinely fun.
